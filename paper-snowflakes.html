DIY Paper
Snowflakes

Real snowflakes have six points, and so do these. One square of paper, a few folds and some small cuts.

How to make it

  1. Make a square.Fold one corner of a sheet of printer paper down to the opposite edge and trim off the extra strip. Unfold: you have a square.
  2. Fold a triangle, then fold it in half.Fold the square corner to corner into a triangle, then fold that triangle in half so the long edges meet.
  3. Fold into thirds.Bring the right third forward and the left third back so the edges line up into a cone. Six layers now meet at the point. Trim the uneven tail straight across.
  4. Cut small shapes.Cut triangles, notches and curves along both folded edges. Leave part of each fold uncut so the snowflake holds together.
  5. Unfold gently.Open it slowly and press it flat under a heavy book for an hour.
  6. Hang them.Tape them to a window, or string several on jute twine as a garland. Warm candles below make them glow in the evening.
Tip: Thin paper cuts more cleanly than cardstock. Small, sharp scissors make the fine details much easier.
