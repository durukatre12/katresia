Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how katresia ("we") handles information in connection with this website and the katresia Pinterest account (pinterest.com/katresiastudio), including the internal tool we use to manage our own Pinterest content through the Pinterest API.

1. Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect personal information from visitors. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

2. Pinterest API usage

Our internal tool connects only to the katresia Pinterest account, with the account owner's authorization through Pinterest's official OAuth flow. It is used to create and schedule our own Pins and boards and to read analytics for our own account. It does not access, collect or store data of other Pinterest users. Access tokens are stored securely on our own systems, are never shared with third parties, and can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings.

3. Affiliate links

Some links we share are affiliate links. When you click them, the destination store or affiliate network may use cookies under its own privacy policy to attribute a purchase. We do not receive your personal or payment details.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Emails are kept only as long as needed to respond. You can ask us to delete any correspondence at any time.
