DIY Cinnamon Stick
Ornaments

Three cinnamon sticks, jute twine and a dried orange slice. They smell like Christmas, cost almost nothing and double as gift toppers.

How to make it

  1. Bundle 3 cinnamon sticks.Pick three straight sticks about 3–4 inches (8–10 cm) long and line them up side by side. Thick cassia sticks hold up better than the crumbly Ceylon kind.
  2. Tie with jute twine.Wrap jute twine around the middle of the bundle 3–4 times and tie a tight double knot. Leave the tails long, you will use them in the next step.
  3. Add a dried orange slice.Tie the orange slice to the front with the twine tails, or fix it with a dab of hot glue. A small eucalyptus sprig behind it adds green.
  4. Hang on the tree.Tie a 6-inch (15 cm) loop of twine and hang it on the tree, a window or a doorknob.
Star version: Cross three sticks at their centers into a six-point star, hot-glue the middle and wrap it with twine. Gift topper version: Tie a bundle of three sticks and one dried orange slice onto a kraft paper package with jute twine, then add a kraft tag. It is the easiest wrapping upgrade there is.
