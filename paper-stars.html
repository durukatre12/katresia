How to make it

  1. Cut eight squares.For one star, cut 8 equal squares from cardstock or sturdy paper, e.g. 4 × 4 inches. Mixing two or three colors looks great.
  2. Fold a kite.Fold each square in half diagonally and unfold. Then fold two neighboring edges in to meet that center crease, so the square becomes a kite shape.
  3. Fold it in half.Fold each kite in half along its long center line, so you get a slim folded point. Crease firmly.
  4. Join the points.Glue the points together side by side, all tips facing out, adding one at a time until the 8 points close into a full star.
  5. Make more for a garland.Repeat in different colors and sizes. Punch a small hole in one point of each star.
  6. Hang them.Thread on jute twine for a garland, hang single stars in a window at different heights, cluster them on a branch, or use one big star as a tree topper.
Tip: Bold colors photograph best: try tomato red, hot pink and lemon yellow on a plain wall, or one color in three shades for an ombre row.
