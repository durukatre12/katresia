DIY Woven
Paper Stars

The classic Scandinavian (Froebel) star: four paper strips, a woven square and sixteen points. Tricky the first time, easy by the third.

How to make it

  1. Cut 4 paper strips.Each star needs 4 strips of the same size. Ready-cut star strips are easiest; if you cut your own, keep the length about 30 times the width, for example ½ inch × 15 inches (1.3 × 38 cm).
  2. Fold each strip in half.Fold all four strips in half and trim the ends to a point. Each folded strip now has two "legs".
  3. Weave them into a square.Lay the folded strips at right angles so each one hooks around the legs of the next, like a little woven basket. Pull all the ends gently until the square in the middle is tight and flat.
  4. Fold the flat points.Take the top leg of each pair, fold it back over itself, then fold it in half diagonally to make a triangle point and tuck the end under the woven square. Do all four, then flip the star and repeat: eight flat points.
  5. Make the 3D points.Take one of the remaining legs, twist it into a small cone with the good side out, and push the end through the square next to it. Repeat around the star on both sides for eight cone points.
  6. Trim and hang.Snip the leftover ends close to the star. Thread fine twine through one point and hang them in a window, on the tree or on a wrapped gift.
Tip: Paper that is too thick will not twist into cones. Thin, smooth paper strips work best, and a slightly damp fingertip helps the last points slide through.
