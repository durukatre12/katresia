DIY Salt Dough
Ornaments

Flour, salt and water. That is the whole recipe. Stamp them, bake them and hang them with twine or velvet ribbon.

How to make it

  1. Make the dough.Mix 1 cup all-purpose flour with ½ cup table salt. Add ½ cup warm water little by little and knead for 5–10 minutes until smooth and not sticky.
  2. Roll and cut.Roll the dough about ¼ inch (6 mm) thick. Cut shapes with cookie cutters: stars, circles, trees or hearts.
  3. Press a pattern and poke a hole.Press a rubber stamp, lace or a leaf into each shape. Poke a hanging hole near the top with a straw.
  4. Bake low.Bake at 250°F (120°C) for 2–3 hours, flipping halfway, until hard and completely dry. Or let them air-dry for 2–3 days.
  5. Seal and hang.Leave them natural white or paint them, then seal with a clear varnish so they last for years. Thread jute twine or velvet ribbon through the hole.
Tip: Thinner ornaments dry faster and puff less. If the dough cracks while baking, your oven is too hot. Lower it and bake longer.
